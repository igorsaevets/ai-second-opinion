## 1.119.2 — 2026-11-08

Two fixes on the default path. A Grok tool may now run up to 300 s before the inner watchdog stops the run (90 s cut working web searches), and a free API voice whose request gets no response headers sends that request once more inside the same call (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on NVIDIA's free endpoint lost 10 of its last 24 reviews that way).

- **Grok's inner watchdog waits 300 s for every tool** (was 90 s since 1.117.0). Measured on the author's machine: 64 Grok web searches, median 58.7 s, the longest 172.3 s, 5 past 90 s; in a 2026-11-09 round the 90 s limit fired as a web search completed (94.6 s) and the whole review was lost under «stayed pending». `GROK_TOOL_SILENCE` is 300 s (`ORCH_GROK_TOOL_SILENCE` still overrides) and `GROK_TOOL_SILENCE_BY_TOOL` (`web_search` 300 s) is a per-tool floor the default never lowers. A tool that completed as the watchdog fired is now recorded as such (`watchdog_raced: true`, «completed as the watchdog fired», its own `diagnose()` entry) instead of as a hang. Suite `suite_r175_grok_tool_limits`.
- **A free API voice re-sends one request that got no response headers.** When the headers of a request do not arrive within 300 s, the same request is sent once more inside the call; the conversation grows only after a successful round, so the copy carries the pages already read. Once per call and only on channels whose `cost` is `free`: a paid request with no headers may still be queued and billed upstream, so paid channels never re-send. Deadline and stall cuts are never re-sent. The record gains `headers_resent` (`after_rounds`, `at_s`, `answered`) and `headers_waits_s`, `retries` counts the re-send, and `diagnose()` gains two entries for `NO RESPONSE HEADERS within` (plain, and after the re-send). A copy that fails too costs at most 300 s more, and 1.119.0's free-voice rule then applies unchanged (silence: no retry, no fallback). Measured: DeepSeek V4.1 Flash on NVIDIA's free endpoint, 24 rounds since 2026-10-31, 11 answered, 10 ended on «no response headers within 300 s» (6 on the first request). A live probe on 2026-11-09 got HTTP 504 from NVIDIA's own gateway after ~302 s for a small and a large request alike, so a longer limit would not help, and in such an hour the copy fails too: the re-send rescues a single dropped request; `headers_resent[].answered` in the records shows how often. Suite `suite_r176_headers_resend`.

## 1.119.0 — 2026-11-02

Three source-tree features bundle together. A free voice that fails inside its 25-minute window now retries or falls back inside the same round, not after it. Chat panel seats scaffold ships off by default (overlay flips them on). Overlay channels can carry a `valid_through` date that flips them off automatically.

- **Free-voice IN-ROUND retry+fallback rule** (R172 И-2; operator's rule 2026-10-31). `orchestrate.main` uses `concurrent.futures.wait(FIRST_COMPLETED)` with a nested in-round pool; a free primary's failure is classified by `_classify_free_failure(r, elapsed_s, 1500 s)` into `past_window` / `silence` / `transient` + delay / `quota` / `other`. A known transient (429, 5xx) retries the primary once in-round with a 60 s / 30 s delay bounded by the remaining window; a known refusal (`provider.quota`, `provider.free-limit`) or an unknown-but-real failure dispatches the fallback at once in-round. Silence (clean exit, no error, no answer) and failure past the 25-minute window end the voice without a retry and without a fallback — the operator's rule verbatim «silence or past 25 min: no fallback». The marker `free_inround` on the primary record blocks the after-round AUTO-RETRY and the after-round fallback from re-firing. Paid and subscription voices keep the after-the-round fallback, unchanged. Env overrides for CI and probe worlds: `ORCH_FREE_INROUND_WINDOW` (window seconds), `ORCH_FREE_INROUND_RETRY_429` (429 delay seconds), `ORCH_FREE_INROUND_RETRY_5XX` (5xx delay seconds). The rule was live-verified on a cheap panel on 2026-11-02: 8/8 behaviours matched the expected branch, 0/4 false positives on paid voices. Suite: `suite_r172_i2_free_inround` (29 checks a–k).
- **Chat panel seats scaffold** (R173 И-3). Four new channels — `claudeaiseat` (chatseat, CDP), `cldesktopseat` (uiaseat, UI Automation), `cgptseat` (chatseat), `cgptdeskseat` (uiaseat) — are present in the kit with `distribution: local` and `enabled: false`; an overlay enables them. `--panel chat` refuses to run without `--chat claude|chatgpt|both`. Drivers live in the author's `seats/` directory and are excluded from the kit tree — a chat seat is operated through the author's own browser or desktop app and is not a general-purpose channel. The kill-switch env `R173_SEAT_FORBID_LIVE` leaves `chat_slot_spent: False` so a refusal does not count a chat slot. The chat panel is exempt from the free-voice in-round rule (D5). Suites: `suite_r173_i2_chat_panel_scaffold` (wired), `suite_r173_i3_seats_topology` (a–d3).
- **`valid_through` self-expiry in routing** (R174 И-1). `routing._valid_through_expired(ch, today=None)` returns the ISO date when today > `valid_through` (lexical YYYY-MM-DD compare), None otherwise (shape guard rejects non-string, wrong length, wrong separator, non-digit components). `initial_plan` AND-s expiry into `enabled`, writes `valid_through` + `expired_on` into the plan, and prepends a `why` line naming the date. `default_enabled` is not touched by expiry — a spend-gate can still tell "off by default" from "on but expired". Overlay channels with `valid_through` revert to off automatically the day after the date — the mechanism IS the reminder, no code change, no manual step. Suite: `suite_r174_valid_through` (18 checks a–s).

Panel counts stay at the 1.118.0 numbers (cheap 6, standard 12; `--ask` 4; chat channels off by default). Selftest in SOURCE after this release: 2517/2517 PASS (R174 И-1's 18 + R173 И-3's 8 + R172 И-2's 29 added since 1.118.2).

## 1.115.2 — 2026-10-24

What the cheap panel found in 1.115.1: the reading list still sent you to Nemotron's answer, and the free-allowance memory kept the wrong second.

- **`HANDOFF.md` no longer counts Nemotron's answer among the files to read.** 1.115.1 marked every Nemotron channel `never_read` for `--ask`, but a panel's reading list still counted that file in its totals, and its ready-to-paste resume block said «read every file». ## 1.112.6 — 2026-10-17

Route fixes from the review panel on 1.112.5. Each sentence below was run on 1.112.5 with a paid channel in the slot it turns off, and started a channel the speaker had excluded or never named.

- **«X вместо A и B» replaces both.** «grokbuild вместо kimik3 и codex» started codex; on the standard panel «grokbuild вместо codex и kimik3» ran the paid kimik3. An infix «вместо» joins only the names linked to it by «и» / «and» / a comma; after a comma, «вместо» starts a new clause.
- **The English word «or» no longer selects a channel group.** «only codex or grokbuild» started three paid OpenRouter channels: «or» was an alias of the `openrouter` group. A function word (or, and, the, no, not, и, а, но, или, в, на, с, …) never names a channel or a group.
- **Negations after the name, far from it, or in words the grammar did not list are caught.** «codex не нужен, возьми grokbuild», «добавь grokbuild, codex не трогай», «а codex пропусти», «codex в сторону», «запрети codex», «никакого codex», «за исключением codex», «don't ever use codex», «don't you dare use codex», «codex запрещён», «use neither codex nor grokbuild» and «не‐используй codex» (a Unicode hyphen) all started codex. «за исключением», «исключая», «excluding», «пропусти», «не нужен» are exclusions now; for the other shapes the second check reads the whole clause, also the words after the name, and stops the run with the way to write it. That check still never changes a plan.
- **A replaced channel takes every paid twin of its group with it, also the ones before it.** «grokbuild вместо orspark13cont» with the free Spark not ready ran the paid spark13cont; «grokbuild вместо orwonder, не используй mcwonder10cont» ran wonder10. Twins whose cost is `mid`, `cheap`, `metered` or `expensive` count as paid, so «codex вместо orgemini38flash» also leaves agy38flash off.
- **A free channel you name that is not ready here STOPS the run** instead of handing its voice to its paid twin with a PAID line («добавь kimik3free» without its key ran the paid kimik3). Name the paid channel to pay for it, or leave the free one out.
- **«why not skip codex» excludes codex.** 1.112.5 added it: the double-negation rule read «not skip» as «use». «почему бы не» the same.
- **«только не: codex», «only not: codex», «только не? codex» exclude codex.** Punctuation after «не» made 1.112.5 run codex ALONE.
- **Every n't form excludes:** «shan't use codex», «hasn't», «mightn't», «ain't», «isn't to use codex» added codex.
- **«no one but X», «nobody but X» and «ничего — кроме X» (with a dash) mean only X.** They ran the panel without X (the paid kimik3 then carried the Kimi voice). «without excluding X» keeps X (it dropped the free Spark and ran the paid spark13cont).
- **No more false stops on** «не исключительно codex, но и grokbuild», «добавь grokbuild и не забудь codex», «don't exclude the codex»: 1.112.5 refused them, they run as written.
- **A run-time fallback whose model the route replaced stays off.** «grokbuild вместо ocmimo26flashfree» left the metered mimov26flash armed as mimov26pro's fallback, so a mimov26pro failure would have brought the replaced model back, paid. The plan prints why it is off.
